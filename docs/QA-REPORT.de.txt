Roadmap, proposals B5 and B6 of 03.10.2026: a gamepad (sticks, shoulder buttons, Start for pause, the menus first with mouse
and touch, then the controller) and vibration on hits, dashes and boss blows (Android, switchable). Built from the browser's
Gamepad API; tested with a faked controller, not with real hardware.

1) The controller (ui/gamepad.js, Input.updatePad)
   - Left stick moves; right stick aims and fires (like the aim stick on a touch screen), the right trigger or bumper fires; A or
     the left bumper dashes; X the Nova; Y the Singularity; Start pauses and resumes. The dead zone (0.2) is cut out of the middle
     of a stick so that it does not jump from 0 to 0.2.
   - Menus, pause, the upgrade choice and the end screen: the D-pad or the left stick moves a ring of focus to the nearest button
     in that direction (the top layer only: an open dialog, else the last visible screen); a held direction repeats after 0.4
     s; A presses what is in focus; B goes back (the Esc path); Y rerolls the upgrades. The ring is removed while a run is played.
   - Nothing runs while no controller is connected.

2) Vibration (Settings, Vibration; default on)
   - A hit, a dash and the end of a run: the controller's motors (dual-rumble, or pulse where that is all there is) and, where
     the screen is touched (Android Chrome), a buzz of the phone (30, 14 and 120 ms; navigator.vibrate). Never on a desktop.

3) Found by the first test run
   - The import of rumble was missing in main.js: the frame loop reported a ReferenceError ("a hit makes the controller rumble",
     "Start pauses the run" failed). Fixed.

4) Tests
   - Full QA gamepad (a faked controller): the ring of focus, A and B in the menus, the left stick moves the drone, the right
     stick aims up and fires, A dashes, a hit rumbles, Start pauses and resumes, no controller leaves no ring; vibration-phone:
     a hit buzzes, with the setting off it stays still.

5) Results 3.23.0 (headless Chromium, software WebGL; npm run release-check)
   - npm run release-check (covers 3.21.0 to 3.23.0, which were built one on the other): all steps ok except the determinism
     step, which reported one changed hash, "data-tables": the table of default settings is part of it and the new Vibration
     setting changed it (key "Oh": fc675a28... -> a00c1356...). That is meant: the fixture was updated with --update; all fifteen
     other cases (every simulation, the statistics, the layouts, the planner) are unchanged. Time of the chain: 32 min 15 s.
     (Earlier in the night the full QA also caught the missing import of rumble, see 3.)

6) Open doubts
   - Not tested on real hardware: an Xbox or PlayStation pad, the buttons of non-standard pads (the browser maps most pads to the
     standard layout), the vibration of a real phone, a pad in Safari. The focus ring has no explanation on screen (no "press A"
     hints); the touch buttons of a phone stay visible when a pad is used.

RIFTLINE v3.20.0 – ULTRA LOOK
Base: v3.19.0 · Build r3200a1005a-r1 · 06.10.2026

Asked by the owner (05.10.2026): "it's all looking like a kids game, we need more professional style"; answered with
"ultra sure": an opt-in Ultra preset. The pictures of 3.19.0 were looked at first: flat even light, saturated colours, a soft
blob under everything, no material. Ultra changes the light and the picture, not the models (a pass on the models comes
later, with a reference from the owner).

1) What Ultra does (render/look.js; Settings, Graphics: Auto, High, Ultra, Saver)
   - A filmic tone curve (ACES) with an exposure per biome, so that the dark places stay readable and the Cryo Vault does
     not burn out (yard 2.2, works 1.9, vault 1.25, marsh 1.9, void 2.1).

     shadows, so they fall on a plane that draws only shadows (ShadowMaterial) just above it. The walker, the enemies and
     the props cast; glows, rings, sprites and beams do not. The blob shadows are not drawn.
   - The scene goes into a 4x multisampled half-float target and through one grading pass: 10 % less saturation, cool
     shadows and warm lights, the tone curve and display transform, a vignette and a fine moving film grain. The HUD (a
     2D overlay) is not graded.
   - Only with Ultra: Auto, High and Saver draw exactly as before (checked in the test below).

2) Looked at (tests/shots/biomes-pc-ultra against tests/shots/biomes-pc)
   - Blackout City: first attempt olive-brown and washed out (exposure 1.18 not applied: the exposure of the renderer
     did not reach the pass; moved into the pass as a uniform); with 2.2 a moody noir, the hexagons of the floor visible,
     hard soft-edged shadows under the pillars, a vignette. Ember Works and Cryo Vault: filmic, contrast and colour kept.
   - Found while testing: a pass written with ShaderMaterial must not include the tone-mapping chunks itself (three adds
     them: "toneMappingExposure redefinition"); the blob shadow pool shows its mesh again every frame, so Ultra hides it
     in the pass.

3) Tests
   - Full QA section `ultra`: the Ultra button; saving; the look is on (tone curve, shadow map, sun, catcher, no blobs); 190
     solids cast; no shader or GL error; back on High nothing is left; no page errors. biome-shots can show Ultra

RIFTLINE v3.19.0 – AMBIENT BLOOM
Base: v3.18.2 · Build r3190a1005a-r1 · 06.10.2026

The direction of the owner (05.10.2026): "I like the sound for example in the mobile game Osmos. Basically I just want
high quality (stereo) ambience, so a nice subtle melody, ambient effect sounds etc. but so that it's noticeable and nice.
This should be distinct for each biome." Synthesis first (reference clips from the owner follow later). This is the
first step: a quiet generative melody on every calm theme, in stereo, with a voice for each place.

1) What it is (audio/sound.js: BLOOM, BLOOM_VOICE, calmBloom, the ping-pong bus)
   - At four places of the bar single notes may bloom; the chance is highest when it is quiet and falls as the fight
     swells. A note is a chord tone or one of two colour notes of the place, in the register of the place, with its own
     place in the room, and rings out for 2 to 7 s through the ping-pong echo and the room of the music.
   - Voices: Blackout City a felt piano, Ember Works struck pipes (low, inharmonic partials), Cryo Vault glass (high), Toxin
     Marsh a kalimba, Void Core sine bells that sing a fifth and sink away. Notes are deterministic (hash of bar, step,
     biome), optional voices of the music budget, partials never above 12 kHz.
   - The ping-pong echo is new (SoundEngine.ping): two delays of 0.43 s feeding each other, one heard hard left and one
     hard right. Where the browser has no stereo panner the bloom is simply absent.
   - The boss tracks, the menu music, the sounds and the atmospheres are unchanged.

2) Measured (offline stereo renders, 8 bars of each calm theme at intensity 0.2, with and without the bloom)
   - The bloom adds 16 to 37 % of the level of the theme (yard 0.31, works 0.37, vault 0.33, marsh 0.24, void 0.16): noticeable
     and under the theme. The sound is wider (energy of the difference of the channels against that of their sum): yard
     0.13 -> 0.21, works 0.17 -> 0.24, vault 0.11 -> 0.19, marsh 0.07 -> 0.13, void 0.19 -> 0.21.
   - 12 to 41 tones per 8 bars (a note is two to four tones). Registers: Ember Works 110 to 1380 Hz, Blackout City 330 to
     5950 Hz (partials), Cryo Vault 520 to 10700 Hz, Toxin Marsh 175 to 5440 Hz, Void Core 310 to 4750 Hz.
   - tools: `node tools/qa.js scape-preview music` renders stereo MP3s (calm-quiet 40 s at intensity 0.15, calm busy, boss)
     for each biome into tests/shots/scapes to listen to without playing.

3) Tests
